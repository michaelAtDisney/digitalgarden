## Core Content

This source outlines a central lore mechanic within the [[wiki/entities/project-wallick-disney-history\|Project Wallick]] narrative, revealing that music in the Disney parks is not merely entertainment, but a highly engineered form of [[wiki/concepts/sonic-reality-manipulation\|sonic reality manipulation]]. Harmonious, meticulously structured musical scores function as an active [[wiki/concepts/containment-protocol\|containment protocol]], acting as a [[wiki/concepts/containment-protocol\|sonic stabilizer]] to reinforce a fragile and weakened dimensional fabric. 

Conversely, [[wiki/concepts/chaotic-music\|chaotic music]] and [[wiki/concepts/sonic-dissonance\|sonic dissonance]] behave as weapons capable of causing severe spatial and temporal disruptions. This is demonstrated by [[wiki/entities/the-bears\|the bears]] in [[wiki/entities/frontierland\|Frontierland]], whose off-key, drunken singing directly triggered a [[wiki/concepts/reality-fracture\|reality fracture]] that damaged the temporal fabric of the area. Additionally, the text introduces [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Ritm]], a scientist who dismisses the park's music as unscientific and emotional melodic noise that interferes with his instruments, ironically unaware that this very [[wiki/concepts/dimensional-support-system\|dimensional support system]] prevents his entire reality from collapsing. This document provides foundational information regarding the field of [[wiki/concepts/music-in-project-wallick\|Music in Project Wallick]].

## Main Points

- Music in the [[wiki/entities/project-wallick-disney-history\|Project Wallick]] universe is a powerful force of [[wiki/concepts/sonic-reality-manipulation\|sonic reality manipulation]] used to control and contain dimensional instability.
- The discordant, chaotic singing of [[wiki/entities/the-bears\|the bears]] acts as an acoustic weapon, which directly caused a severe [[wiki/concepts/reality-fracture\|reality fracture]] in the local temporal fabric of [[wiki/entities/frontierland\|Frontierland]].
- The highly structured, harmonious musical scores played throughout the Disney parks function as a crucial [[wiki/concepts/containment-protocol\|containment protocol]] to keep the fragile reality from collapsing.
- [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Ritm]] represents scientific ignorance of this phenomenon, dismissing the vital, stabilizing ambient music as annoying melodic noise that interferes with his sensitive instruments.
