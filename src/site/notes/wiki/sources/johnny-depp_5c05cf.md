## Summary

This character profile details the secret role of [[wiki/entities/johnny-depp\|Johnny Depp]] (John Christopher Depp II) within the Project Wallick universe. Rather than being a mere civilian actor, Depp operates as an unwitting rogue temporal agent who uses unauthorized [[wiki/concepts/temporal-travel\|temporal travel]] to teach historical pirates chivalry. His unsanctioned timeline alterations create severe fractures in reality, manifesting as a localized [[wiki/concepts/weakened-reality-matrices\|weakened reality]] at the [[wiki/entities/pirates-of-the-caribbean\|Pirates of the Caribbean]] attraction. The [[wiki/entities/s-e-a\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers (S.E.A.)]] is forced to continuously cover up these disruptions as public ride updates, while [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Ritm]] remains completely oblivious to Depp's role due to his own intellectual arrogance.

## Key Points

- **Rogue Temporal Activity**: [[wiki/entities/johnny-depp\|Johnny Depp]] is an eccentric artist operating as an unaffiliated rogue temporal agent, traveling through time to alter pirate history.
- **Reality Instability**: Depp's temporal meddling bypasses [[wiki/concepts/s-e-a-protocols\|S.E.A. protocols]], causing localized [[wiki/concepts/weakened-reality-matrices\|weakened reality]] and widespread temporal fractures throughout the park.
- **S.E.A. Containment**: The [[wiki/entities/s-e-a\|S.E.A.]] must constantly repair the temporal damage and disguise Depp's historical alterations as controlled theme park updates for [[wiki/entities/pirates-of-the-caribbean\|Pirates of the Caribbean]].
- **Blind Spot**: Due to intellectual arrogance, [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Ritm]] remains blind to Depp's reality-altering excursions, instead incorrectly blaming Dr. Seeker for the park's chronological instability.
- **Anachronistic Style**: Depp projects himself in the style of [[wiki/entities/captain-jack-sparrow\|Captain Jack Sparrow]] but frequently wears modern, out-of-place accessories like sunglasses and wristwatches during his trips.
