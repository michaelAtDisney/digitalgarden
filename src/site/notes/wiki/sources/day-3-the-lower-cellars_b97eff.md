# SUB-ETHER AUDIT REPORT: THE LOWER CELLARS - Summary

- Original file: [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Veggie Veggie Fruit Fruit/Day 3 - The Lower Cellars\|50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Veggie Veggie Fruit Fruit/Day 3 - The Lower Cellars.md]]

## Core Content

This [[wiki/concepts/sub-ether-field-log\|sub-ether field log]], authored by Chief Courier [[wiki/entities/robert-bobby-curtis\|Robert "Bobby" Curtis]] of the [[wiki/entities/s-e-a-central-information-desk\|S.E.A. Central Information Desk]], details a critical operational hazard beneath Epcot's [[wiki/entities/the-land-pavilion\|The Land Pavilion]]. Addressed to [[wiki/entities/evelyn\|Dr. Evelyn Reed]] of the [[wiki/entities/temporal-containment-corps\|Temporal Containment Corps]] as part of the [[wiki/entities/day-3-the-lower-cellars\|Day 3 - The Lower Cellars]] audit, the report documents an unauthorized reactivation attempt by [[wiki/entities/general-knowledge\|General Knowledge]] in mothballed Vault B, situated beneath the [[wiki/entities/sunshine-seasons\|Sunshine Seasons]] dining area and the historic [[wiki/entities/kraft-foods\|Kraft Foods]] offices. 

The General attempted to command defunct [[wiki/entities/kitchen-kabaret\|Kitchen Kabaret]] figures, such as [[wiki/entities/mr-dairy-drummond\|Mr. Dairy Drummond]], [[wiki/entities/fiesta-fruit\|Fiesta Fruit]], and [[wiki/entities/hamm-&-eggs\|Hamm & Eggs]]. However, these mechanical assets were gutted in 1994, and their hydraulic systems were integrated directly into the agricultural pumps for [[wiki/entities/living-with-the-land\|Living with the Land]]. Despite the lack of physical figures, the original 1982 [[wiki/entities/master-acoustic-drive-cylinder\|master acoustic-drive cylinder]] is fully powered, back-feeding raw [[wiki/concepts/bio-harmonic-resonance\|bio-harmonic resonance]] into the pavilion's grid, destabilizing the localized [[wiki/concepts/reality-lock\|reality-lock]], and triggering anomalous audio calls at [[wiki/entities/soarin-around-the-world\|Soarin' Around the World]]. The General now intends to broadcast a [[wiki/concepts/harmonic-directive\|harmonic directive]] through the agricultural flumes, which could induce destructive biological reactions.

## Main Points

- The physical animatronic figures from [[wiki/entities/kitchen-kabaret\|Kitchen Kabaret]] are long gone; in 1994, their hydraulic and pneumatic systems were hard-wired directly into [[wiki/entities/living-with-the-land\|Living with the Land]]'s agricultural pump array.
- The 1982 [[wiki/entities/master-acoustic-drive-cylinder\|master acoustic-drive cylinder]] remains active and is currently back-feeding raw [[wiki/concepts/bio-harmonic-resonance\|bio-harmonic resonance]] into the pavilion's electrical lines.
- This resonance is causing severe operational anomalies, including a breakdown in the localized [[wiki/concepts/reality-lock\|reality-lock]] that has triggered historical PA announcements at [[wiki/entities/soarin-around-the-world\|Soarin' Around the World]].
- [[wiki/entities/general-knowledge\|General Knowledge]] plans to broadcast a [[wiki/concepts/harmonic-directive\|harmonic directive]] directly into the irrigation flumes.
- If executed, this audio broadcast could trigger an uncontained, synchronized biological reaction across the pavilion's active greenhouses.
