## Core Content

This source is an official [[wiki/entities/project-wallick-disney-history\|Project Wallick]] communiqué written by [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Algernon G. Ritm]] to [[wiki/entities/the-project-team\|The Project Team]]. In the message, Ritm expresses intense frustration and disappointment regarding the rejection of [[wiki/entities/algernon-g-ritms-portrait\|Algernon G. Ritm's Portrait]] from the hallowed halls of the [[wiki/entities/s-e-a\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers (S.E.A.)]]. The [[wiki/entities/s-e-a-president\|S.E.A. President]] formally rejected the portrait on the grounds that Ritm has not yet achieved the "necessary level of status" required for such an honor. Ritm vehemently refutes this decision, accusing S.E.A. leadership of a profound lack of vision and of operating in an antiquated [[wiki/concepts/pre-ritm-era\|Pre-Ritm Era]]. He explains that his portrait is far more than a simple likeness; it is a visual codification of [[wiki/concepts/ritms-theories\|Ritm's Theories]] that seek to unravel the fabric of existence. Despite this setback, Ritm remains undeterred and confident that his scientific brilliance will eventually hang in its rightful place.

## Main Points

- [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Algernon G. Ritm's]] self-commissioned [[wiki/entities/algernon-g-ritms-portrait\|portrait]] has been formally rejected for inclusion in the [[wiki/entities/s-e-a\|S.E.A. Halls]].
- The [[wiki/entities/s-e-a-president\|S.E.A. President]] cited Ritm's lack of the "necessary level of status" as the official justification for the rejection.
- Ritm expresses immense disdain for S.E.A.'s antiquated metrics of rank and prestige, claiming they operate in a [[wiki/concepts/pre-ritm-era\|Pre-Ritm Era]].
- The portrait is described by Ritm as a visual representation of [[wiki/concepts/ritms-theories\|Ritm's Theories]] that seek to unravel the fabric of existence.
- Ritm vows to [[wiki/entities/the-project-team\|The Project Team]] that this rejection is merely a temporary setback and that his genius will eventually force the organization to display the painting.
