## Summary

This source document details an ongoing ideological conflict within [[wiki/entities/project-wallick-disney-history\|Project Wallick]] regarding the "World of Color" attraction. [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Algernon G. Ritm]] has drafted a highly technical memorandum redefining the artistic water show as a [[wiki/entities/world-of-color\|Hydro-Dimensional Flux Emitter]] tied to the [[wiki/concepts/d-i-s-n-e-y-matrices\|D.I.S.N.E.Y. matrices]]. Ritm heavily criticizes his predecessor, [[wiki/entities/dr-n-itely-show\|Dr. N. Itely Show]], for attempting to guide this portal using musical theory. However, [[wiki/entities/evelyn\|Dr. Evelyn Reed]] uncovers archival notes indicating Show successfully conceptualized the portal's elements as [[wiki/concepts/liquid-harmony\|liquid harmony]] and [[wiki/concepts/starlight-made-manifest\|starlight made manifest]], warning Ritm that a purely clinical approach lacks the musical understanding necessary to stabilize the gateway. Additionally, the file highlights a deep personal rivalry, referencing Ritm's upcoming negative reaction in [[wiki/entities/ritm-painting-and-response\|Ritm Painting and Response]] regarding a portrait of Dr. Show.

## Key Points

- **Portal Identification**: Professor Ritm scientifically classifies the "World of Color" water show as a dimensional portal named the "Hydro-Dimensional Flux Emitter" or "Chromatic Gate," operating within the broader [[wiki/concepts/d-i-s-n-e-y-matrices\|D.I.S.N.E.Y. matrices]].
- **Ideological Clash**: Ritm dismisses Dr. Show's previous theatrical and music-based research as "intellectual laziness," planning to implement rigid, data-driven mathematical protocols to contain the portal.
- **Archival Counterpoint**: Dr. Evelyn Reed discovers that Dr. Show viewed the water as [[wiki/concepts/liquid-harmony\|liquid harmony]] and the lights as [[wiki/concepts/starlight-made-manifest\|starlight made manifest]], suggesting the portal operates on non-traditional, emotional, and musical variables.
- **System Failure Risk**: Evelyn warns Ritm that his purely clinical, logical approach is "missing a beat," failing to account for the core musical nature required to interact safely with the gate.
- **Academic Rivalry**: The documents reference a personal feud, noting that Ritm will be deeply offended by Dr. Show receiving a portrait, directly linking this file to [[wiki/entities/ritm-painting-and-response\|Ritm Painting and Response]].
