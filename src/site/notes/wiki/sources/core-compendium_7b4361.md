## Core Content

The **Core Compendium** establishes the primary narrative foundation for Project Wallick. In this universe, Disney theme parks serve as elaborate cover-ups for [[wiki/entities/project-wallick-disney-history\|The D.I.S.N.E.Y. Project]], a top-secret scientific initiative that uses the parks' attractions to contain and manage temporal and dimensional anomalies. These anomalies occur naturally on geographical thin points known as [[wiki/concepts/weakened-reality-matrices\|weakened realities]]. 

Managed clandestinely by [[wiki/entities/s-e-a\|The Society of Explorers and Adventurers]], the containment efforts are plagued by internal academic rivalries—such as that between the arrogant [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm]] and his former college friend [[wiki/entities/herr-professor-doktor-maximus-raumkopf\|Herr Professor Doktor Maximus Raumkopf]]—while brilliant assistants like [[wiki/entities/evelyn\|Dr. Evelyn Reed]] perform the uncredited labor. The compendium outlines key anomalies and containment systems, including the mutagenic [[wiki/entities/pixie-dust\|pixie dust]] of [[wiki/entities/the-matterhorn\|The Matterhorn]], reality-stabilizing foodstuffs developed by the [[wiki/entities/bureau-of-gustatory-stability\|Bureau of Gustatory Stability]], and the profound influence of [[wiki/concepts/music\|Music]] as both a source of chaos and a tool for spatial containment.

## Main Points

- **The True Mission**: Disney theme parks are containment complexes built intentionally on highly volatile [[wiki/concepts/weakened-reality-matrices\|weakened realities]].
- **The Role of Gastronomy**: Consumables like the [[wiki/concepts/dole-whip\|Dole Whip]] are synthesized to stabilize the biological energy fields of guests against dimensional shifting.
- **Rogue Time Travel**: Unauthorized temporal excursions by [[wiki/entities/johnny-depp\|Johnny Depp]] and [[wiki/entities/dr-grant-seeker\|Dr. Grant Seeker]] have created severe fractures, forcing containment cover-ups.
- **Acoustic Mechanics**: Harmonious scores serve as containment fields (such as [[wiki/entities/world-of-color\|World of Color]]), while chaotic, discordant tunes (such as the Bears' singing) can directly rupture spatial integrity.
