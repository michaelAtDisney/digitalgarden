# S.E.A. protocols

## Definition

The S.E.A. protocols are the official scientific guidelines and operational boundaries established by the [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] (Society of Explorers and Adventurers). These protocols are designed to safely manage anomalous activity and strictly preserve temporal integrity.

## Key Characteristics

- **Anomaly Mitigation**: Establishes standard procedures for detecting, analyzing, and containing anomalous events to prevent public exposure or destabilization.
- **Temporal Preservation**: Defines strict boundaries regarding time-altering actions to protect historical continuity and prevent paradoxes.
- **Scientific Rigor**: Enforces high ethical and experimental standards, serving as the benchmark against which reckless or unauthorized temporal exploration is measured.

## Applications

- **Temporal Integrity Control**: Used by [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] agents to monitor and intervene in timeline disruptions caused by rogue actors operating outside official oversight.
- **Attraction Conversion**: Serves as the regulatory framework for converting uncontrolled, chaotic timeline interferences—such as those generated by [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Johnny Depp\|Johnny Depp]]—into structured, controlled public attractions.
- **Operational Benchmarking**: Utilized by scholars and scientists like [[Professor Ritm\|Professor Ritm]] to evaluate the safety and scientific responsibility of exploratory missions.
