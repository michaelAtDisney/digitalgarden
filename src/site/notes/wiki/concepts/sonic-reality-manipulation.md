# sonic reality manipulation

## Definition

Sonic reality manipulation is a metaphysical phenomenon within the [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]] universe where specific sound frequencies, acoustic resonance, and musical compositions are utilized to alter, stabilize, control, or disrupt the physical and temporal fabric of reality. Rather than serving a purely aesthetic or entertainment purpose, the audio and music broadcasted throughout the parks act as a physical force capable of maintaining environmental stability or causing localized destruction.

## Key Characteristics

- **Physical and Temporal Agency**: Soundwaves function as a tangible force that can interact with, bind, or warp physical space and temporal progression.
- **Dual Functionality**: Sound can act either as a stabilizing anchor to lock reality in place or as a destructive resonance capable of tearing the local fabric of space-time.
- **Operational Masking**: Leverages the mundane operational infrastructure of Disney theme parks—such as area background music loops, animatronic show soundtracks, and parade audios—to conceal active reality-binding operations from the public.

## Applications

- **Anomalous Containment**: Utilizing specific audio loops to suppress anomalies and prevent the expansion of localized tears in reality.
- **Entity Suppression**: Calming, neutralizing, or directing supernatural entities within the park, such as [[the bears\|the bears]], using specialized acoustic frequencies.
- **Environmental Stabilization**: Running continuous acoustic broadcasts to maintain structural and temporal integrity in highly volatile sectors like [[wiki/entities/frontierland\|Frontierland]].
- **Dimensional Portal Manipulation**: Stabilizing and shaping active gateways like the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/World Notes/World of Color\|World of Color]] by utilizing musical frequencies and emotional resonance to control responsive physical mediums like water and light.
