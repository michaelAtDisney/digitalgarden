# containment protocol

## Description

Within the framework of [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]], a containment protocol is the systematic deployment of highly organized, harmonious music that acts as a sonic stabilizer to soothe, reinforce, and maintain weakened dimensional boundaries. These meticulously orchestrated scores—ranging from park parades to ambient soundtracks—serve as an acoustic defense system and vital [[dimensional support system\|dimensional support system]] to neutralize localized temporal decay and counteract [[reality fracture\|reality fractures]]. This continuous broadcast of perfect harmony stands in direct opposition to chaotic, off-key music, which can act as a weapon by creating sonic dissonance capable of tearing the temporal fabric. Though crucial for maintaining spatial integrity, this invisible defensive grid is dismissed by [[Professor Ritm\|Professor Ritm]] as unscientific melodic noise, leaving him unaware that it is the primary countermeasure preventing total dimensional degradation.

## Definition

Within the framework of [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]], a containment protocol is the systematic deployment of highly organized, harmonious music designed to soothe, stabilize, and reinforce a weakened or fractured reality. These meticulously orchestrated musical arrangements act as a sonic stabilizer to prevent dimensional degradation.

## Key Characteristics

- **Continuous Broadcast:** Transmitted without interruption through park parades, localized area audio loops, and live shows to maintain constant coverage.
- **Harmonious Orchestration:** Relies on "perfect scores"—highly precise musical selections and arrangements engineered to soothe existential tension.
- **Invisible Defensive Grid:** Operates as a non-physical protective barrier, utilizing sound waves to secure structural integrity against dimensional collapse.

## Applications

- **Reality Stabilization:** Deployed in areas suffering from existential instability to patch and reinforce weakened dimensions.
- **Environmental Management:** Used in themed parks and public spaces to maintain a safe, controlled environment where the boundaries of reality might otherwise fluctuate.
- **Proactive Defense:** Serving as an invisible grid to preemptively secure zones vulnerable to dimensional breaches or anomalies.
