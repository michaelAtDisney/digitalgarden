# Fortitudo Per Gustum

## Definition

**Fortitudo Per Gustum** (Latin for "Strength Through Taste") is the official motto of the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Bureau of Gustatory Stability/Bureau of Gustatory Stability\|Bureau of Gustatory Stability]]. It represents the agency's pragmatic, scientific doctrine that culinary experiences and taste-based stabilizers can be actively used to preserve, anchor, and protect the structural integrity of local reality against ontological decay or anomalous drift.

## Key Characteristics

* **Pragmatic Flavor Science**: Prioritizes the physical and metaphysical properties of flavor profiles as viable tools for reality stabilization.
* **Ontological Anchoring**: Grounded in the theory that taste—one of the most visceral human sensory experiences—can act as a foundational anchor to keep local dimensions tethered to standard baseline reality.
* **Institutional Alignment**: Reflects the operational philosophy championed by leadership, such as [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Commodore Flavius M. Palate\|Commodore Flavius M. Palate]], emphasizing rigorous scientific application of culinary agents over purely theoretical metaphysical defenses.
* **Clinical Reframing**: Transmutes seemingly whimsical food items into highly disciplined, clinical containment measures designed to safeguard public safety.
## Applications

* **Anomalous Mitigation**: Guiding the deployment of engineered food items designed to neutralize localized reality fluctuations.
* **Stabilizer Development**: Serving as the foundational design philosophy behind synthetic and natural stabilizers, most notably the specialized deployment of the [[Dole Whip\|Dole Whip]] in threatened zones.
* **Agency Identity**: Utilized on official seals, documentation, and headquarters architecture within the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Bureau of Gustatory Stability/Bureau of Gustatory Stability\|Bureau of Gustatory Stability]] to reinforce the mission of defense through culinary preservation.
