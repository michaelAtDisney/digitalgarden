# World Notes

## Definition

**World Notes** is a specialized organizational system and subdirectory designed to store environmental, institutional, and geographic context for historical research projects, specifically utilized within the Disney History project. It functions as a centralized repository for world-building data, historical settings, and background details that frame primary narratives, providing the situational backdrop necessary to understand major events and individual journeys.

## Key Characteristics

- **Contextual Anchoring**: Focuses on the environmental, institutional, and geographic conditions of a given historical era.
- **Supportive Structure**: Acts as a background layer that supports and enhances primary narrative structures such as biographical timelines and chronological events.
- **World-Building Focus**: Collects and organizes details regarding political climates, organizational structures, physical locales, and systemic factors that influenced historical outcomes.
- **Multidimensional Data**: Integrates diverse situational data points (e.g., studio geography, industry standards, institutional policies) to create a cohesive research environment.

## Applications

- **Grounding Historical Narratives**: Provides researchers with the immediate environmental and cultural settings needed to explain why certain decisions or events occurred at specific points in time.
- **Contextualizing Character Arcs**: Offers a reference point for understanding the systemic and institutional constraints or opportunities experienced by key historical figures.
- **Project Organization**: Serves as a dedicated directory within research databases to separate broad environmental data from specific event timelines or person-specific profiles.
