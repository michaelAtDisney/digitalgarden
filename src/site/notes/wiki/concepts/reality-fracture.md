# reality fracture

## Definition

A **reality fracture** is a severe tear or destabilization in the local temporal and spatial fabric of the universe. Within the context of Project Wallick, these fractures are triggered by extreme acoustic dissonance and chaotic, uncoordinated sound waves, which act as accidental weapons against dimensional integrity.

## Key Characteristics

- **Acoustic Genesis**: Triggered by high levels of chaotic and discordant acoustic energy rather than conventional physical force.
- **Spatio-Temporal Instability**: Results in severe localized disruptions, causing time and space to warp or behave unpredictably.
- **Self-Propagating Volatility**: Once initiated, the fracture destabilizes the surrounding environment, requiring continuous active intervention to prevent complete localized collapse.
- **Dimensional Vulnerability**: Acts as a breach in the boundaries separating distinct dimensional planes or timelines.

## Applications

- **Hazard Assessment**: Used by researchers to identify areas at risk of structural collapse due to prolonged exposure to uncontrolled acoustic frequencies.
- **Strategic Containment**: Dictates the deployment parameters for specialized stabilizing equipment to secure regions affected by temporal chaos.
- **Acoustic Defense Design**: Informs the development of acoustic dampening systems to prevent accidental tears in highly sensitive zones.
