# Ritm's Theories

## Definition

Ritm's Theories are a suite of complex scientific frameworks articulated by [[Professor Algernon G. Ritm\|Professor Algernon G. Ritm]] concerning the fundamental nature of physical reality. These theories claim to unravel the underlying fabric of existence, representing a revolutionary departure from traditional scientific understanding and establishing a new era of discovery. 

## Key Characteristics

- **Fundamental Nature**: Explores and seeks to explain the core fabric of existence and reality.
- **Visual Encoding**: The theories are claimed to be visually encoded directly within [[Algernon G. Ritm's Portrait\|Algernon G. Ritm's Portrait]], a self-commissioned painting.
- **Paradigm Shift**: Positions itself as a revolutionary transition away from traditional scientific methodologies, establishing a clear boundary with the [[Pre-Ritm Era\|Pre-Ritm Era]].
- **Structural Pillar**: Serves as a foundational scientific cornerstone within the world of [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]].

## Applications

- **Project Wallick Integration**: Provides the core scientific principles and theoretical backing for developments and operations within [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]].
- **Visual Decipherment**: Serving as a subject of study where researchers analyze [[Algernon G. Ritm's Portrait\|Algernon G. Ritm's Portrait]] to decode and extract Ritm's hidden scientific formulations.
- **Scientific Revisionism**: Used as a framework to re-evaluate and transition away from traditional scientific doctrines.
