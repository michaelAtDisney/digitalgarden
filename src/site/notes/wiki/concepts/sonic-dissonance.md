# sonic dissonance

## Definition

Sonic dissonance is a destructive acoustic phenomenon characterized by discordant, off-key, and chaotic frequencies that possess the capacity to damage the fabric of reality. Within the Project Wallick narrative, this phenomenon is not merely an auditory or aesthetic issue but an active physical force capable of tearing at temporal and spatial structures.

## Key Characteristics

- **Spatial and Temporal Distortion**: Directly interacts with and destabilizes the local continuum, leading to structural instability in reality itself.
- **Discordant Composition**: Triggered by chaotic, off-key, and non-harmonious audio frequencies.
- **Amplification Sensitivity**: The destructive capabilities of the phenomenon scale exponentially when the sound source is artificially amplified or projected over large areas.

## Applications

- **Dimensional Hazard**: Acts as an unconventional, highly dangerous environmental hazard that can spontaneously compromise secure zones.
- **Reality Fracturing**: Utilized or triggered (often unintentionally) to initiate localized reality fractures, as demonstrated by the amplified, discordant singing of [[the bears\|the bears]] which directly caused a severe reality fracture within [[wiki/entities/frontierland\|Frontierland]].
