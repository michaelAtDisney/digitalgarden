# harmonic directive

## Definition

A **harmonic directive** is a targeted audio broadcast designed to command, control, or synchronize biological and mechanical systems that have been structurally integrated with legacy attraction hardware. 

## Key Characteristics

- **Medium-Based Transmission:** Often transmitted through fluid or structural mediums (such as flume water) to reach integrated biological and mechanical components.
- **Hybrid System Interface:** Capable of simultaneously influencing both organic matter (such as crops) and mechanical components (such as animatronics or pump systems).
- **Rhythmic Synchronization:** Induces a unified, often syncopated physical response from responsive systems tuned to the specific frequency of the broadcast.

## Applications

- **The Land Pavilion Coordination:** Planned for use by [[General Knowledge\|General Knowledge]] to transmit command signals through the greenhouse flume water of [[Living with the Land\|Living with the Land]]. 
- **Legacy Hardware Activation:** Interacts with the flume's pump and aeration systems—which are powered by cannibalized, responsive Kitchen Kabaret animatronic parts—causing agricultural crops to react in a synchronized, rhythmic motion.
