# Extraterrestrial and Magical Anomalies

## Definition

Extraterrestrial and Magical Anomalies is an unorthodox academic discipline that bridges the gap between folklore, magical theory, and astronomical science. The field posits that magical occurrences, mythical events, and supernatural substances are actually tangible phenomena of cosmic or extraterrestrial origin. 

## Key Characteristics

- **Cosmic Attribution of Magic**: It treats magical elements, such as the creation of [[Pixie Dust\|Pixie Dust]], not as supernatural occurrences violating physical laws, but as exotic cosmic matter or energy originating from outer space.
- **Interdisciplinary Synthesis**: It combines elements of traditional folklore and magical theory with rigorous astronomical and geological science.
- **Anomalous Investigation**: Adherents focus on proving that highly unusual earthly landmarks, such as the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Matterhorn\|Matterhorn]], and legendary phenomena possess distinct cosmic origins or extraterrestrial connections.

## Applications

- **Anomalous Geology**: Analyzing unusual mountain ranges and geographic formations to detect signs of extraterrestrial manipulation or cosmic impact.
- **Astro-Mythology**: Re-evaluating ancient folklore, myths, and magical texts to extract historical data on astronomical events.
- **Cosmic Material Analysis**: Investigating rare, seemingly magical substances to determine their chemical and extraterrestrial compositions.
