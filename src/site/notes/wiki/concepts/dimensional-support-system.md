# dimensional support system

## Definition

The **dimensional support system** is a functional infrastructure deployed within theme parks that utilizes precisely choreographed audio to maintain the stability of local realities. It operates as an ongoing containment protocol, utilizing every layer of park audio—ranging from subtle ambient background tracks to major parade scores—to counteract and offset the constant threat of temporal fractures. 

## Key Characteristics

- **Acoustic Stabilization**: Uses carefully orchestrated soundscapes and musical frequencies to anchor dimensional boundaries.
- **Continuous Containment**: Operates non-stop to mitigate and neutralize recurring temporal anomalies and fractures.
- **Deceptive Design**: Seamlessly integrated into the park environment as entertainment, leading outside observers to mistake the system's vital stabilizing frequencies for mere background music or melodic noise.

## Applications

- **Theme Park Reality Anchoring**: Employed in high-traffic, multi-dimensional park environments to ensure that localized reality pockets do not collapse.
- **Temporal Fracture Mitigation**: Dynamically scales audio outputs (such as shifting from ambient loops to high-energy parade scores) to reinforce structural stability during periods of elevated dimensional stress.
