# dimensional folding

## Definition

Dimensional folding is an advanced spatial manipulation technique that enables travel, transit, or physical interaction across different dimensional layers by bending and compressing the fabric of space. 

## Key Characteristics

- **Spatial Compression**: Decreases the metric distance between two disparate points by folding the intervening dimensional fabric.
- **Inter-dimensional Traversal**: Facilitates safe passage and communication between the standard physical dimension and alternative planes, such as the Atomium dimension.
- **Structural Synchronization**: Requires external stabilizing mechanisms to prevent dimensional shearing or spatial collapse during folding operations.
- **Proprietary Origin**: Originates as a highly specialized science native to the Atomium dimension, later introduced to Earth's scientific groups.

## Applications

- **Secret Global Transit**: Serves as a core technical methodology for the [[Society of Explorers and Adventurers\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers]] (S.E.A.) to operate a covert, rapid transport network across the globe.
- **Anomalous Containment**: Used to isolate dangerous entities or unstable materials within folded, inaccessible dimensional pockets.
- **Co-technological Stabilization**: Deployed alongside [[Temporal-Harmonic Anchors\|Temporal-Harmonic Anchors]] to secure pathways across both spatial and temporal boundaries.
