# public-facing containment protocols

## Definition

Public-facing containment protocols are the formalized safety and security procedures utilized by the [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] to neutralize or stabilize supernatural anomalies while keeping them hidden in plain sight from the general public. These protocols allow the Society to maintain safety and regional stability without causing public panic.

## Key Characteristics

- **Hidden in Plain Sight**: Containment efforts are masked as everyday commercial products, public services, or tourist novelties to avoid raising suspicion.
- **Panic Mitigation**: Designed specifically to neutralize supernatural threats without revealing the existence of anomalous phenomena to the general populace.
- **Bureaucratic Oversight**: Typically managed by specialized divisions within the [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]], such as the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Bureau of Gustatory Stability/Bureau of Gustatory Stability\|Bureau of Gustatory Stability]], which integrates containment mechanisms into consumable goods.
- **Regional Stability**: Focused on preserving local normalcy and preventing external disruptions in areas experiencing active supernatural activity.

## Applications

- **Anomalous Stabilization via Consumables**: Utilizing food products as delivery systems for containment agents, such as the distribution of the [[Dole Whip\|Dole Whip]] under the [[W.H.I.P.\|W.H.I.P.]] protocol to neutralize regional anomalies.
- **Disguised Operations**: Masking security personnel and containment equipment as standard service workers, vendors, or culinary staff under the direction of figures like [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Commodore Flavius M. Palate\|Commodore Flavius M. Palate]].
