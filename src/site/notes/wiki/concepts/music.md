# Music

## Definition

In this narrative universe, **Music** is not merely an aesthetic or acoustic phenomenon, but a fundamental underlying force that directly governs and influences dimensional stability. Depending on its composition, coherence, and performance, music can act either as a violent catalyst for reality degradation or as a highly effective stabilizing agent in containment operations.

## Key Characteristics

- **Dimensional Influence**: Operates as a cosmic resonance that directly interacts with the structural integrity of localized space-time and reality boundaries.
- **Chaotic Destabilization**: Discordant, chaotic, or uncoordinated musical expressions—such as the drunken singing of the Country Bears—disrupt environmental baselines, leading to localized reality fluctuations and dimensional destabilization.
- **Harmonious Stabilization**: Highly structured, rhythmic, or harmonious music—such as a formal show score—functions as a anchoring mechanism, reinforcing dimensional boundaries and neutralizing anomalous fluctuations.

## Applications

- **Containment Protocols**: Utilized by specialized agencies to secure volatile zones and anomalies, relying on continuous, structured audio loops or scores to maintain spatial integrity.
- **Acoustic Reality Anchoring**: Employed in unstable dimensional sectors to prevent reality bleeding and to keep physical environments from collapsing.
