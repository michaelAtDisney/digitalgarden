# Frontierland temporal anomaly

## Definition

The Frontierland temporal anomaly is a localized chronological disruption that permanently froze the region of [[wiki/entities/frontierland\|Frontierland]] in the year 1887. It completely halted the flow of time within the affected area, leaving its environment and inhabitants perpetually suspended in the late 19th century.

## Key Characteristics

- **Localized Chronological Stasis**: The anomaly is strictly confined to the geographic boundaries of [[wiki/entities/frontierland\|Frontierland]], completely halting the passage of time inside the zone while the external world continues to progress normally.
- **Permanent Suspension**: Time within the boundary is locked specifically in the year 1887, preventing any natural aging, decay, or progression.
- **Experimental Origin**: The anomaly was triggered by the actions and experiments of [[Dr. Cadence Ritm\|Dr. Cadence Ritm]] during an attempt to resolve a destructive rampage of his genetically modified bears.

## Applications

While the anomaly is a hazardous chronological disruption rather than a deliberate tool, it presents unique scenarios for study and observation:
- **Temporal Physics Research**: It serves as a rare, stable subject for researchers studying localized stasis fields and the mechanics of temporal containment.
- **Involuntary Historical Preservation**: The anomaly provides an unchanging, real-time archive of late-19th-century life, technology, and architecture, preserved exactly as it was in 1887.
