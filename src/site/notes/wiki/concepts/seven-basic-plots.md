# Seven Basic Plots

## Definition

The **Seven Basic Plots** is a foundational narrative theory stating that all stories, regardless of their complexity or medium, are built around seven recurring archetypal patterns. Popularized by author and theorist Christopher Booker, this framework posits that human storytelling is governed by deep psychological structures. In advanced narrative design frameworks, such as the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/World Notes/7×7 Story Matrix\|7×7 Story Matrix]], these seven plots serve as the core structural and thematic building blocks for systematic story generation and analysis.

The seven archetypal plots are:
1. **Overcoming the Monster**: A protagonist sets out to defeat an antagonistic force that threatens the community.
2. **Rags to Riches**: An unremarkable protagonist gains wealth, power, or status, loses it, and grows to regain it with true maturity.
3. **The Quest**: A protagonist (and companions) travels to acquire a distant, invaluable object or reach a specific destination, overcoming obstacles along the way.
4. **Voyage and Return**: A protagonist is thrust into an unfamiliar world, experiences its wonders and dangers, and returns home transformed by the journey.
5. **Comedy**: A story characterized by confusion, miscommunication, and escalating social chaos that ultimately resolves into harmony and union.
6. **Tragedy**: A protagonist with a fatal flaw falls from grace, ending in destruction or death due to their choices.
7. **Rebirth**: An unredeemed protagonist falls under a dark spell or negative influence before being redeemed and transformed by a positive force.

## Key Characteristics

* **Archetypal Universality**: The seven plots represent deeply ingrained cultural and psychological patterns that resonate across different eras, languages, and cultures.
* **Structural Blueprint**: Each plot dictates a predictable progression of tension, conflict, climax, and resolution.
* **Combinatorial Flexibility**: While often studied as distinct, isolated structures, these plots can be combined or overlaid. In the context of [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/World Notes/7×7 Story Matrix\|7×7 Story Matrix]] design, they act as both the horizontal and vertical axes of a matrix to systematically map narrative variations.
* **Thematic Influence**: Beyond driving plot events, each pattern carries inherent thematic undertones (e.g., redemptive growth in Rebirth, hubris in Tragedy) that shape the moral and emotional resonance of a story.

## Applications

* **Systematic Worldbuilding and Game Design**: Narrative designers use these archetypes as structural templates to quickly draft diverse quest lines, character arcs, and faction conflicts.
* **[[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/World Notes/7×7 Story Matrix\|7×7 Story Matrix Generation]]**: By placing the Seven Basic Plots on both axes of a matrix, creators can systematically explore how a primary structural driver (horizontal axis) morphs when influenced by a secondary thematic undertone (vertical axis).
* **[[Hybrid Storytelling\|Hybrid Storytelling]]**: Combining two or more of the seven basic plots allows writers to craft multi-layered narratives, such as a "Voyage and Return" quest that is structurally driven by a "Tragedy."
* **Narrative Analysis**: Literary critics and analysts use the framework to deconstruct complex multi-POV novels, cinematic universes, and interactive games into their fundamental storytelling components.
