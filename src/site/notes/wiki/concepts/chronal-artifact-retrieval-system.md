# chronal artifact retrieval system

## Definition

The **chronal artifact retrieval system** is a clandestine operations protocol designed to locate, secure, and recover dangerous temporal artifacts. Disguised as a public Indiana Jones themed attraction, the system operates covertly to stabilize reality and mitigate the risks of temporal fractures.

## Key Characteristics

- **Covert Infrastructure**: Operates under the guise of a highly themed amusement attraction to mask high-energy temporal signatures and unusual logistics.
- **Strategic Location**: Situated on the reclaimed grounds of the former [[Dinosaur Institute\|Dinosaur Institute]] to leverage existing research and temporal anomalies localized to that area.
- **Reality Stabilization**: Focuses primarily on retrieving volatile chronal objects that threaten the integrity of the timeline.
- **Clandestine Management**: Run by a dedicated group of covert researchers who manage both the physical recovery missions and the accompanying public cover-up initiatives.

## Applications

- **Temporal Asset Recovery**: Safely locating and extracting artifacts displaced across time.
- **Fracture Prevention**: Preventing localized temporal collapses by removing active chronal anomalies from the timeline.
- **Containment and Cover-up**: Masking state-of-the-art temporal technology and anomalous events as theatrical special effects and ride mechanics to maintain public deniability.
