# S.E.A. Temporal Protocols

## Definition

The S.E.A. Temporal Protocols are a comprehensive suite of regulatory guidelines and standardized operating procedures established to govern all temporal experimentation, observation, and research within the S.E.A. organization. 

## Key Characteristics

- **Safety-First Framework**: Designed primarily to prevent catastrophic temporal failures, causal loops, and severe chronological instability.
- **Interference Mitigation**: Prohibits unauthorized chronological interference to preserve the integrity of the established timeline.
- **Strict Oversight**: Developed and enforced under the supervision of [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Frank Walker\|Frank Walker]] to align with the operational limits of temporal instrumentation.
- **Operational Alignment**: Integrated directly with the development, deployment, and execution of [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/The Timekeeper\|The Timekeeper]].

## Applications

- **Experimental Governance**: Serving as the mandatory legal and operational compliance framework for scientists conducting temporal research.
- **Device Safeguards**: Providing the core safety parameters and operational boundaries for executing runs with [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/The Timekeeper\|The Timekeeper]].
- **Observational Containment**: Ensuring that field research, specifically techniques like [[Closed-Loop Temporal Observation\|Closed-Loop Temporal Observation]], does not inadvertently disrupt past or future events.
