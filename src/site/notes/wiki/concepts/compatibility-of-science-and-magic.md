# Compatibility of Science and Magic

## Definition

The **Compatibility of Science and Magic** is a philosophical and methodological framework proposing that scientific inquiry and magical phenomena are not mutually exclusive. Instead of viewing them as opposing forces, this theory posits that science and magic coexist, interact, and can ultimately be used to explain and validate one another. 

## Key Characteristics

* **Methodological Dualism**: Champions the combined use of empirical, rigorous scientific research alongside subjective intuition and traditional folklore to study unexplained phenomena.
* **Mutual Explanation**: Asserts that magical occurrences can be systematically analyzed and understood through scientific principles, while magic can offer explanations for anomalies that conventional science fails to address.
* **Anti-Pragmatic Stance**: Rejects the rigid, purely materialistic pragmatism that dismisses non-physical or magical explanations out of hand.

## Applications

* **Investigation of the Unknown**: Providing a conceptual framework for analyzing anomalous substances and phenomena, such as researching the potential extraterrestrial origins of Pixie Dust.
* **Alternative Scientific Inquiry**: Allowing researchers to leverage historical folklore and intuitive insights as valid starting points for empirical laboratory testing.
