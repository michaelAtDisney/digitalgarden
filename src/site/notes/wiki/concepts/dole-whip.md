# Dole Whip

## Description

[[Dole Whip\|Dole Whip]], officially designated as the Weakened Harmonizing Inter-dimensional Procedure (W.H.I.P.), is a pineapple-flavored soft-serve treat engineered as a stabilized gustatory agent. Formalized under the leadership of [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Commodore Flavius M. Palate\|Commodore Flavius M. Palate]] and distributed by the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Bureau of Gustatory Stability/Bureau of Gustatory Stability\|Bureau of Gustatory Stability]], this protocol standardizes the precise deployment of biochemically active energy sinks to protect human cells from the physical degeneration caused by exposure to [[Weakened Reality\|Weakened Reality]]. The procedure serves as a primary administrative defense to ensure the public safety and structural integrity of [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] parks, bridging the gap between volatile supernatural anomalies and bureaucratic order. Originally developed during the research of [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]] to exploit weakened dimensional boundaries, this substance allows unsuspecting guests to safely walk through anomalous park zones without realizing they are undergoing active biochemical treatment.
