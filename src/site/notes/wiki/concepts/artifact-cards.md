# artifact cards

## Definition

Artifact cards are physical or digital narrative tools used in experience design to deliver modular, lore-rich overlays and thematic information to guests. These cards act as tangible extensions of a themed environment's story world, prompting specific interactions or revealing hidden layers of history. 

## Key Characteristics

* **Modular Storytelling**: Delivers structured, collectible story fragments that guests can discover and piece together non-linearly.
* **Tangible Worldbuilding**: Serves as physical or digital touchpoints that bridge the gap between the guest and the themed environment.
* **Systematic Generation**: In frameworks like the [[7x7 Story Matrix\|7x7 Story Matrix]], they are generated using structured narrative pairings to ensure cohesive and diverse lore.
* **Guest Agency**: Encourages self-guided exploration, curiosity, and active participation within an interactive space.

## Applications

* **Themed Entertainment**: Providing guests with physical keepsakes that unlock deep-lore elements in theme parks, escape rooms, or immersive theater.
* **Museum Exhibitions**: Offering interactive, self-guided narrative layers that reveal the historical or cultural context of physical exhibits.
* **Alternate Reality Games (ARGs) & LARPs**: Serving as gameplay items, clues, or status trackers that players can trade, collect, and analyze.
