# emotional arcs

## Definition

Emotional arcs represent the structured trajectories of feelings and psychological states experienced by characters or guests over the duration of a narrative, journey, or designed experience. Within hybrid storytelling and themed environments, these arcs provide the "emotional texture" or thematic flavor that overlays a story's physical actions or primary quest, mapping out the rise and fall of psychological tension.

## Key Characteristics

- **Structured Trajectories**: Emotional arcs map deliberate sequences of psychological states (such as "Hope → Struggle → Loss") to guide the participant's internal journey.
- **Thematic Overlay**: They serve as the "emotional texture" of an experience, ensuring that physical actions (e.g., moving through rooms, solving puzzles) are imbued with specific narrative meaning and feeling.
- **Tension Management**: They allow designers to intentionally orchestrate the building, peaking, and releasing of narrative tension and emotional resonance.
- **Multisensory Alignment**: These arcs serve as a blueprint for experience designers to synchronize environmental effects, lighting, soundscapes, narrative cues, and operational pacing with the intended emotional state of the guest.

## Applications

- **Experience Design**: Utilized within frameworks like the 7x7 Story Matrix to map out the guest's emotional journey step-by-step through themed spaces.
- **Hybrid Storytelling**: Bridging the gap between physical mechanics (e.g., game rules, ride operations) and narrative depth by establishing a parallel track of feeling.
- **Operational Pacing**: Assisting operations teams in scheduling the timing of staff interactions, environmental transitions, and show elements to align with the target emotional response.
