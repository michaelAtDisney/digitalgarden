# Pre-Ritm Era

## Definition

The **Pre-Ritm Era** is a derogatory term coined by [[Professor Algernon G. Ritm\|Professor Algernon G. Ritm]] to characterize the contemporary state of mainstream scientific inquiry and exploration. According to Ritm, this era is defined by a superficial focus on institutional prestige, bureaucratic hierarchy, and outdated scientific models, standing in sharp contrast to the advanced paradigm of understanding inaugurated by his own work.

## Key Characteristics

- **Institutional Stagnation**: Characterized by an obsession with rank, titles, and social prestige within established scientific bodies, such as [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]], at the expense of genuine scientific progress.
- **Scientific Ignorance**: Marked by mainstream science's inability or refusal to recognize the fundamental, deeper truths of nature that Ritm claims to have uncovered.
- **Egotistical Framing**: Serves as a self-aggrandizing historical marker, dividing scientific history into a "primitive" past (the Pre-Ritm Era) and an enlightened future defined by Ritm's own discoveries.

## Applications

- **Rhetorical Dismissal**: Used by [[Professor Algernon G. Ritm\|Professor Algernon G. Ritm]] as an ideological tool to dismiss criticism from mainstream academics, labeling his detractors as relics of a lesser intellectual age.
- **Contextualizing Ritm's Theories**: Applied within Ritm's personal writings and lectures to frame his research not merely as an incremental discovery, but as an epoch-defining revolution that renders previous scientific frameworks obsolete.
