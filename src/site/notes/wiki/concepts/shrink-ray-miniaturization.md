# Shrink Ray Miniaturization

## Definition

Shrink Ray Miniaturization is a controversial containment and mitigation method proposed by [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm\|Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm]] to manage localized reality bleed-throughs. The method outlines a technical procedure to physically shrink theme park guests down to match the environmental scale of an affected area, specifically proposed for [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Toy Story Land/Toy Story land\|Toy Story Land]]. 

## Key Characteristics

- **Scale Equalization**: Aims to resolve ontological and physical discrepancies by altering the physical dimensions of human observers rather than modifying the anomalous environment.
- **High-Risk Technical Complexity**: Involves direct physical alteration of human subjects, making it highly controversial and safety-critical.
- **Theatrical Counterposition**: Opposed by traditional containment philosophies that favor passive structural solutions, such as constructing oversized environmental props (e.g., giant-sized toys) to simulate scale differences.
- **Academic Friction**: The rejection of this method by governing bodies remains a primary source of professional discord and friction within the research notes of its creator.

## Applications

- **Reality Bleed-Through Management**: Proposed as a direct intervention to preserve the narrative and spatial integrity of [[Weakened Reality\|Weakened Reality]] zones where scale incongruity threatens public exposure or safety.
- **Dimensional Alignment**: Potential application in matching human explorers to micro-environments or pocket dimensions where standard human scale prevents safe entry or interaction.
