# Music in project Wallick

## Definition

Within the [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]] narrative, music in the Disney parks is not merely a source of entertainment, but a highly engineered discipline of sonic reality manipulation. Meticulously structured and harmonious musical scores function as an active containment protocol, generating a stabilizing sonic field that reinforces a fragile, weakened dimensional fabric.

## Key Characteristics

* **Active Containment Protocol**: Harmonious and structured melodies function as physical stabilizers, actively holding together local dimensional boundaries that would otherwise collapse.
* **Destructive Discordance**: Chaotic, off-key, or discordant sounds function as spatial-temporal weapons, capable of causing localized tears and fractures in the fabric of reality.
* **Scientific Obscurity**: The stabilizing frequencies are often misidentified by conventional scientists as mere emotional noise, hiding their critical engineering function from standard instruments.

## Applications

* **Dimensional Stabilization**: The continuous playback of park music serves as a permanent, passive barrier preventing the decay of local spatial dimensions.
* **The Frontierland Incident**: An unintended demonstration of sonic discordance occurred in [[wiki/entities/frontierland\|Frontierland]], where the off-key, drunken singing of the local animatronic bears caused a localized reality fracture, directly damaging the area's temporal fabric.
* **Observation Interference**: [[Professor Ritm\|Professor Ritm]] frequently dismissed the park's musical scores as unscientific, emotional noise that disrupted his scientific equipment, remaining entirely oblivious to the fact that this melodic noise was actively preventing his entire reality from collapsing.
