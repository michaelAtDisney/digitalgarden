# dimensional frequencies

## Definition

Dimensional frequencies refer to the unique vibrational or wave-based signatures emitted by distinct dimensions and interdimensional portals. These frequencies serve as cosmic coordinates or identifiers, marking the boundaries between standard reality and outer temporal zones. 

## Key Characteristics

- **Vibrational Signature**: Every dimension and portal, such as the [[it's a small world Nexus\|it's a small world Nexus]], emits a distinct, identifiable wave pattern.
- **Perceptual Barrier**: Perceiving, detecting, and interfacing with these frequencies is not possible with standard human senses or conventional instruments; it requires a specialized, rare cognitive or physiological aptitude.
- **Boundary Mapping**: These frequencies are critical to charting, identifying, and stabilizing the thresholds that separate different realities.

## Applications

- **Interdimensional Travel**: Navigating and traversing the boundaries that separate our reality from outer temporal zones, such as the [[Project Atomium\|Project Atomium]] dimension.
- **Portal Calibration**: Aligning transport systems and devices with the correct frequency to ensure safe passage through gateways.
- **Dimensional Mapping**: Cataloging different realms based on their unique electromagnetic or vibrational outputs.
