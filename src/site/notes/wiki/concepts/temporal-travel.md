# temporal travel

## Definition

**Temporal travel** is the method of navigating backward through historical timelines to project an individual or consciousness into past eras. 

## Key Characteristics

* **Historical Projection:** Enables active backward navigation and placement into preceding historical periods.
* **Safety Protocol Bypass:** Can be performed independently of standard scientific frameworks, allowing users to bypass safety regulations.
* **Temporal Fracturing:** Unsanctioned and unprotected journeys cause severe damage to the space-time continuum, fracturing the temporal fabric of reality and triggering localized physical instabilities.

## Applications

* **Historical Intervention:** Attempting to alter the actions and behaviors of historical figures. For example, [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Johnny Depp\|Johnny Depp]] utilized an independent means of temporal travel to project himself into past eras to reform historical pirates and influence them to behave more chivalrously.
* **Timeline Disruption:** While not a primary goal, unsanctioned use of this method acts as a catalyst for localized environmental anomalies, such as the ongoing spatial instabilities centering around the Pirates of the Caribbean attraction and the wider theme park.
