# anomalies

## Definition

Anomalies refer to the unexplained, reality-bending phenomena observed within [[Walt Disney Parks and Resorts\|Walt Disney Parks and Resorts]]. Discovered and studied under [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]], these anomalies represent localized deviations in the reality matrix. Rather than being the result of corporate planning or creative "Imagineering," evidence suggests that many of the parks' most famous attractions were intentionally built around these pre-existing, reality-warping fluctuations.

## Key Characteristics

- **Reality-Bending Fluctuations**: They manifest as physical and spatial deviations that disrupt the standard physical laws within localized zones, often linked to [[weakened reality matrices\|weakened reality matrices]].
- **Pre-existing Origins**: These occurrences are not man-made; instead, the parks' locations were selected specifically because they hosted these pre-existing, naturally occurring anomalies.
- **Hidden Foundation of Attractions**: They serve as the true power source and structural anchor for several prominent park attractions, masked to the public as high-tech special effects.
- **Technological Tracking**: They require advanced detection methods, including [[AI augmentation\|AI augmentation]] and specialized sensory arrays, to identify, map, and monitor their stability.

## Applications

- **Attraction Integration**: Utilizing localized anomalies to power and operate complex, seemingly impossible thematic rides and experiences within [[Walt Disney Parks and Resorts\|Walt Disney Parks and Resorts]].
- **Reality Mapping**: Using diagnostic tools developed by [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]] to track structural weak points in the reality matrix, ensuring the anomalies remain contained and stable.
- **Scientific Analysis**: Providing [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm\|Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm]] and other researchers with raw data on [[localized energy fluctuations\|localized energy fluctuations]] to further understand the nature of reality degradation.
