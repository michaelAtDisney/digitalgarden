# bio-harmonic resonance

## Definition

Bio-harmonic resonance is a phenomenon caused by high-voltage legacy show hardware operating without its native physical outputs, resulting in energy and signal back-feeding directly into modern electrical systems. This feedback loop broadcasts intense musical and mechanical frequencies into the surrounding power grid, causing systematic electrical, software, and biological disruptions.

## Key Characteristics

- **Legacy Back-Feeding**: Triggered when high-voltage, historical entertainment hardware runs decoupled from its original mechanical loads, forcing the generated energy backward into the modern power infrastructure.
- **Frequential Interference**: Induces severe frequency jumps in nearby audio systems and cross-talk throughout automated software and control programs.
- **Biological Mutagenesis**: Capable of physically mutating, over-energizing, or altering the growth patterns of organic systems (such as hydroponic flora) that share mechanical or electrical connections with the active legacy hardware.

## Applications

- **Anomalous Power Grid Diagnosis**: Used to identify and isolate phantom loads, unexpected frequency spikes, and rogue acoustic broadcasts within complex facility grids.
- **Containment and Isolation Engineering**: Informing the design of dampening systems and electrical isolators to prevent uncoupled mechanical assets from corrupting local utility networks.
