# reality-lock

## Definition

A **reality-lock** is a localized technological or metaphysical containment barrier designed to maintain the physical, structural, and chronological integrity of a designated area. By anchoring a specific set of physical laws and temporal parameters, it prevents external fluctuations or historical iterations from bleeding into the active, modern timeline.

## Key Characteristics

* **Chronological Anchoring**: Prevents defunct, retired, or historical concepts, legacy data, and retired entities from manifesting in the active present.
* **Spatial Geofencing**: Operates within highly specific, localized boundaries (such as theme park pavilions or historical sites).
* **Degradation Anomalies**: When a reality-lock begins to degrade, it triggers localized temporal and physical anomalies. These often manifest as sensory overlaps, such as legacy PA security announcements playing in musical harmony with modern audio systems.
* **Data Containment**: Isolates obsolete operational code and legacy programming, preventing legacy network signals from interfacing with modern infrastructure.

## Applications

* **Temporal Isolation at Epcot**: Utilized to prevent retired attraction concepts, extinct pavilion themes, and historical animatronic/character data from leaking into the contemporary park environment.
* **Information Security**: Ensures that retired system architectures and legacy operational protocols remain isolated from modern active systems.
* **Historical Conservation**: Used by specialized teams to safeguard the integrity of current timelines from being overwritten or disrupted by past events.
