# Sub-Ether Field Log

## Definition

A **Sub-Ether Field Log** is a highly classified communication protocol used by field agents operating within the Epcot Sector. These logs serve as secure dispatches to document the physical and temporal state of mothballed attractions, physical vaults, and anomalous temporal events. They are typically transmitted via sub-ether posts to oversight organizations to maintain chronological and structural security.

## Key Characteristics

- **Classified Status**: Highly restricted protocol limited to authorized field agents and monitoring organizations.
- **Sub-Ether Transmission**: Dispatched through specialized sub-ether communication channels, bypassing standard data networks to prevent interception or tampering.
- **Audit Verification**: Used specifically to report on physical audits, containment stability, and the integrity of active reality-locks.

## Applications

- **Infrastructure Auditing**: Documenting the physical degradation, preservation, or entry status of decommissioned attractions and underground storage vaults.
- **Temporal Event Tracking**: Logging occurrences of anomalous temporal activity, chronal slippage, or localized reality destabilization.
- **Status Reporting**: Relaying official containment updates and reality-lock statuses directly to administrative and defensive command centers.
