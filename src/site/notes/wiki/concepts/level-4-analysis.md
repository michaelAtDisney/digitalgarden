# Level 4 Analysis

## Definition

Level 4 Analysis is an elite and rigorous investigative protocol utilized within [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]] for examining highly anomalous artifacts. This scientific and historical scrutiny is reserved for items that display severe deviations from the standard chronological baseline, suggesting direct temporal interference or reality instability. The classification triggers an immediate and intensive investigation to determine the origin, authenticity, and potential timeline-altering implications of the item.

## Key Characteristics

* **High-Level Scientific Scrutiny**: Involves deep forensic, physical, and historical analysis to investigate objects that defy conventional scientific explanation.
* **Chronological Deviation Identification**: Specifically targeted at items showing significant divergence from the [[Expected Historical Record\|Expected Historical Record]].
* **Timeline Threat Assessment**: Focused on identifying the source of temporal interference and determining if the artifact poses a risk of creating a [[Temporal Paradox\|Temporal Paradox]].
* **Authority Triggered**: Typically initiated via direct executive order by senior leadership, such as Professor Algernon G. Ritm, upon the discovery of inexplicably anachronistic objects.

## Applications

* **Anomalous Artifact Investigation**: Applied directly to highly anomalous items like [[wiki/entities/arc-oil-pir-07a\|ARC-OIL-PIR-07A]] to determine their true historical provenance.
* **Reality Instability Analysis**: Used to evaluate whether an object's presence indicates a state of [[Weakened Reality\|Weakened Reality]] in its surrounding environment.
* **Chronological Integrity Verification**: Employed to trace back timeline-altering events and understand the origins of temporal anomalies.
