# Earth

## Description

Earth, also known to some extraterrestrial cultures as Terra, is the home planet of humanity and the birthplace of [[wiki/entities/star-lord-peter-quill\|Peter Quill]], who was born in St. Charles, Missouri. Shortly after his mother's passing in 1988, Quill was abducted from Earth by the [[wiki/entities/ravagers\|Ravagers]], a group of interstellar thieves and mercenaries. This event separated him from his home world for decades. Despite his prolonged absence, Quill remains deeply attached to Earth's culture, particularly the music, pop culture, and media of the 1970s and 1980s, which continues to heavily influence his personality and leadership style.
