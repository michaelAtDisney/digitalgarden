# Frank Walker

## Description

Frank Walker is a brilliant, pragmatic scientist who serves as the Director of the Office of Rapid Deployment and Temporal Transportation within the [[entities/sea\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers (S.E.A.)]]. Driven by a deep need to protect reality following his past failures during [[wiki/entities/project-atomium\|Project Atomium]], Walker acts as a highly competent, results-oriented rival to [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Ritm]]. He possesses a unique ability to interface with dimensional frequencies and has introduced advanced temporal containment technologies to the organization, including the containment of anomalies like the [[wiki/entities/its-a-small-world-nexus\|"it's a small world" Nexus]]. His major achievements include establishing global transport systems and defining crucial [[wiki/concepts/s-e-a-temporal-protocols\|S.E.A. Temporal Protocols]] to prevent reality destabilization.
