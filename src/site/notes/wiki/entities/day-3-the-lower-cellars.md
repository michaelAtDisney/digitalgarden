# Day 3 - The Lower Cellars

## Description

"Day 3 - The Lower Cellars" refers to a critical incident detailed in a field report written by Chief Courier [[wiki/entities/robert-bobby-curtis\|Robert 'Bobby' Curtis]] on August 19, 2026. Addressed to [[wiki/entities/evelyn\|Dr. Evelyn Reed]] of the Temporal Containment Corps, the report outlines an unauthorized reactivation attempt beneath Epcot's The Land Pavilion by an individual known as [[wiki/entities/general-knowledge\|General Knowledge]]. The General attempted to boot up the legacy 1982 Kitchen Kabaret animatronics, which had been gutted in 1994 with their hydraulic and pneumatic systems integrated into the Living with the Land attraction's agricultural pumps. Although the physical figures are gone, the original 1982 master acoustic-drive cylinder remains powered, creating a bio-harmonic resonance that has destabilized the localized reality-lock and triggered anomalous automated PA calls for legacy hostess "Passenger Bonnie Appetit" at Soarin' Around the World. The event remains an active threat as the General intends to broadcast a "harmonic directive" into the greenhouse irrigation flumes, which could trigger a massive, destructive botanical reaction.
