# Cadence Ritm

## Description

Dr. **Cadence Ritm** was a distinguished, mustache-sporting 19th-century member of the [[wiki/entities/s-e-a\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers]] who served as Lead Researcher for the [[wiki/entities/project-for-advanced-mammalian-soldiering\|Project for Advanced Mammalian Soldiering]]. Believing scientific breakthroughs should blend rigorous data with music and romanticism, Ritm partnered with the U.S. government during the [[wiki/entities/apache-wars\|Apache Wars]] to breed super-intelligent soldier bears using positive reinforcement. This experiment ended catastrophically when the bears—who developed a penchant for drinking and singing, ultimately becoming the [[wiki/entities/country-bear-jamboree\|Country Bear Jamboree]]—went on a rampage, killing Mayor [[wiki/entities/pecos-bill\|Pecos Bill]] and establishing the [[wiki/entities/big-thunder-mountain-railroad\|Big Thunder Mountain Railroad]] escape route. The disaster triggered a [[wiki/concepts/frontierland-temporal-anomaly\|Frontierland temporal anomaly]] that froze the region in 1887, causing the Society to bury the records of his "folly" until they were unearthed by [[wiki/entities/evelyn\|Dr. Evelyn Reed]]. This legacy of failure heavily drove his descendant, [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm]], to obsessively pursue [[wiki/entities/project-wallick-disney-history\|Project Wallick]] for familial redemption, while separate cosmic accounts detail Cadence's bizarre bureaucratic entanglement on [[wiki/entities/xandar\|Xandar]] with the [[wiki/entities/bureau-of-gustatory-stability\|Bureau of Gustatory Stability]] following an incident involving [[wiki/entities/star-lord-peter-quill\|Star-Lord (Peter Quill)]].
