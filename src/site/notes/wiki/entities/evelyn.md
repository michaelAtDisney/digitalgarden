# Evelyn

## Description

Dr. Evelyn Reed is a brilliant archival researcher on the [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]] team and a member of [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] (the Society of Explorers and Adventurers). Driven by a genuine curiosity and a strict sense of scientific integrity, she serves as the unsung intellectual engine of the project, often tolerating the pompous behavior of her supervisor, [[Professor Algernon G. Ritm\|Professor Algernon G. Ritm]], solely to retain access to S.E.A.'s exclusive archives and resources. Within the team, she acts as a direct counterweight to Ritm's rigid dogmatism; while Ritm relies on mathematical models, Evelyn uses her practical, no-nonsense approach and dry, sarcastic wit—often communicated through passive-aggressive notes—to challenge his assumptions.

Officially designated as Ritm's research assistant despite being his intellectual equal, Evelyn is the primary driver of the project's actual scientific breakthroughs. It was her persistent archival work that led to the critical discovery of the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Cadence Ritm\|Cadence Ritm]] materials, as well as the unearthing of the [[Country Bears\|Country Bears]] archives, the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Matterhorn\|Matterhorn]] documents, and records from the [[Dinosaur Institute\|Dinosaur Institute]]. Furthermore, by recovering the original notes of [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Dr. N. Itely Show\|Dr. N. Itely Show]], Evelyn identified that artistic elements like music and harmony are key to portal stability, suggesting an intuitive methodology rather than relying solely on Ritm's [[Hydro-Dimensional Flux Emitter\|Hydro-Dimensional Flux Emitter]]. Despite her foundational contributions being routinely dismissed or co-opted by Ritm to soothe his own insecurities, her insights remain essential to containing anomalies across various [[The D.I.S.N.E.Y. Project\|The D.I.S.N.E.Y. Project]] sites.

Beyond her work on Project Wallick, Dr. Reed serves as a key figure within the [[Temporal Containment Corps\|Temporal Containment Corps]], where she coordinates the containment of historical leaks and technological feedback loops triggered by the reactivation of legacy Disney attractions. In this role, she is instrumental in managing localized reality-locks, including her coordination of the emergency response to the bio-harmonic resonance crisis at Epcot's [[The Land Pavilion\|The Land Pavilion]].
