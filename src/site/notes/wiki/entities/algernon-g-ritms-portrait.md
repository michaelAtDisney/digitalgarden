# Algernon G. Ritm's Portrait

## Description

*Algernon G. Ritm's Portrait* is a self-commissioned painting created by or for [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Algernon G. Ritm]] with the original intention of being hung in the S.E.A. Halls. Rather than serving as a standard visual likeness, Ritm designed the portrait to function as a complex, symbolic representation of [[wiki/concepts/ritms-theories\|Ritm's Theories]] and his deep, unconventional understanding of reality. Although the leadership of the [[entities/sea\|S.E.A.]] ultimately rejected the piece, Ritm continues to view the artwork as a masterpiece that will one day hang in its rightful place. Consequently, the painting remains a physical flashpoint for the ongoing conflict between Ritm and the organization.
