# Commodore Flavius M. Palate

## Description

[[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Commodore Flavius M. Palate\|Commodore Flavius M. Palate]] is a traditional, by-the-book leader within the [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] who serves as the director of the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Bureau of Gustatory Stability/Bureau of Gustatory Stability\|Bureau of Gustatory Stability]]. Characterized by his stern expression, neatly trimmed beard, and meticulously tailored naval uniform, he views supernatural and anomalous phenomena through a pragmatic, scientific lens, dismissing their whimsical public appeal in favor of strict safety protocols. Under his command, the Bureau successfully deployed public-facing containment procedures, notably establishing the [[W.H.I.P.\|W.H.I.P.]] (Weakened Harmonizing Inter-dimensional Procedure) protocol using the [[Dole Whip\|Dole Whip]] to stabilize localized spatial anomalies and maintain park integrity. He acts as a stabilizing administrative force, maintaining a professionally strained relationship of polite but firm exasperation with [[Professor Algernon G. Ritm\|Professor Algernon G. Ritm]], whose chaotic behavior he frequently manages—even officially forbidding Ritm from hanging a self-commissioned portrait in the S.E.A. hall. Additionally, Palate is credited with designing the Bureau's official seal and coining its motto, "[[Fortitudo Per Gustum\|Fortitudo Per Gustum]]" ("Strength Through Taste").
