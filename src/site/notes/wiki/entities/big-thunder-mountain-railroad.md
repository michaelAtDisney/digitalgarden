# Big Thunder Mountain Railroad

## Description

[[wiki/entities/big-thunder-mountain-railroad\|Big Thunder Mountain Railroad]] is a physical roller coaster constructed in Frontierland that serves as a containment grid for a massive temporal anomaly. The ride was originally built as a desperate escape route by local residents fleeing the escaped [[wiki/entities/country-bear-jamboree\|Country Bears]], who had recently killed the town's mayor, Pecos Bill. The extreme panic and chaos of this event fractured the local space-time continuum, initiating a localized state of [[wiki/concepts/weakened-reality-matrices\|Weakened Reality]]. This disruption permanently froze the surrounding frontier towns of Rainbow Ridge, Tumbleweed, and Thunder Mesa in time on July 27, 1887.

These predatory [[wiki/entities/country-bear-jamboree\|Country Bears]] were not mere wild animals, but rather highly intelligent super-soldiers engineered during Dr. Cadence Ritm's failed bio-musical experiments.

This secret project to breed the super-intelligent soldier bears was conducted in partnership with the U.S. government during the Apache Wars, utilizing musical training and positive reinforcement that unexpectedly caused the bears to desire alcohol and performance over combat. Following their catastrophic rampage, the [[organizations/society-of-explorers-and-adventurers\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers (S.E.A.)]] buried all records of the incident, which remained lost until their modern rediscovery by Ritm's descendant, [[entities/professor-al-g-ritm\|Professor Al. G. Ritm]].
