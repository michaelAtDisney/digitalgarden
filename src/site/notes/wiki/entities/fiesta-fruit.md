# Fiesta Fruit

## Description

The Fiesta Fruit is a collective group of animatronic characters originally featured in Epcot's defunct Kitchen Kabaret show. During an operational sweep of the lower cellars, [[wiki/entities/general-knowledge\|General Knowledge]] attempted to command them back to their performance stations. However, their physical armatures are completely absent from the cellar. These armatures were dismantled in 1994 and integrated directly into the hydroponic flumes of the Living with the Land attraction.
