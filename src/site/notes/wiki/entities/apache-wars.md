# Apache Wars

## Description

The Apache Wars serve as the historical backdrop for a secret, government-funded experiment conducted by the [[wiki/entities/s-e-a\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers (S.E.A.)]]. Under the leadership of [[wiki/entities/cadence-ritm\|Dr. Cadence Ritm]], this project attempted to breed and train super-intelligent soldier bears to assist in the military conflict. Instead of becoming viable combat assets, the experiment yielded music-loving, alcohol-consuming bears who eventually escaped captivity. This incident and the subsequent escape of the bears caused a severe disruption in the timeline, contributing directly to the onset of [[wiki/concepts/weakened-reality-matrices\|Weakened Reality]] and the formation of the Frontierland anomalies.

Following their escape, the bears killed the town's mayor, Pecos Bill, prompting residents to build the [[wiki/entities/big-thunder-mountain-railroad\|Big Thunder Mountain Railroad]] as an escape route. This event triggered a severe temporal anomaly that permanently froze the neighboring towns of Rainbow Ridge, Tumbleweed, and Thunder Mesa in time on July 27, 1887.

Historically, the Apache Wars were a series of late-19th-century conflicts between the United States military and various Apache nations in the American Southwest, serving as the catalyst for the government's secret partnership with Dr. Cadence Ritm and the [[wiki/entities/s-e-a\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers (S.E.A.)]]. The research was formally named the Project for Advanced Mammalian Soldiering, with [[wiki/entities/cadence-ritm\|Dr. Cadence Ritm]] attempting to train the super-intelligent bears using unconventional music and positive reinforcement rather than traditional military discipline. Following the disastrous escape of the bears, S.E.A. buried all records of the failed project, which remained lost until their eventual rediscovery by Ritm's descendant, Professor Al. G. Ritm.
