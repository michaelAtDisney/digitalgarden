# ARC-OIL-PIR-07A

## Description

`[[entities/arc-oil-pir-07a|ARC-OIL-PIR-07A]]` is the catalog designation for a highly anomalous oil painting discovered within newly acquired `[[entities/imagineering|Imagineering]]` archival materials. The painting portrays a modern figure identical to actor `[[entities/johnny-depp|Johnny Depp]]` dressed in contemporary clothing—such as sunglasses, denim jeans, and a leather jacket—standing amongst historical 18th-century pirates. Its high level of modern realism deviates drastically from the conceptual art style typical of early `[[entities/imagineering|Imagineering]]` drafts for the `[[entities/pirates-of-the-caribbean|Pirates of the Caribbean]]` attraction. Due to its potential connection to temporal manipulation or alternate timelines, Lead Researcher `[[entities/prof-algernon-al-g-ritm|Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm]]` has flagged the painting for Level 4 analysis under the scope of `[[entities/project-wallick-disney-history|Project Wallick]]`.
