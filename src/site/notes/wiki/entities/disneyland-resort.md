# Disneyland Resort

## Description

The [[wiki/entities/disneyland-resort\|Disneyland Resort]], located in Anaheim, California, is the initial geographic subject of [[wiki/entities/project-wallick-disney-history\|Project Wallick]]'s scientific investigation. Under the analysis of researchers like [[entities/algernon-ritm\|Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm]], the site is theorized to host significant reality anomalies where several iconic theme park attractions spontaneously emerged. This phenomenon is hypothesized to be caused by [[wiki/concepts/weakened-reality-matrices\|weakened reality matrices]] rather than conventional construction. Consequently, the resort serves as the foundational case study for proving that the origins of these theme parks, managed by [[wiki/entities/walt-disney-parks-and-resorts\|Walt Disney Parks and Resorts]], are rooted in [[wiki/concepts/localized-energy-fluctuations\|localized energy fluctuations]] rather than pure human design.

To achieve this containment, the resort was strategically constructed as a front to isolate dangerous anomalies from the public, specifically positioned around the [[wiki/entities/the-matterhorn\|Matterhorn]] to hide and secure the naturally occurring roller coaster manifestation.

According to further theories proposed by [[wiki/entities/herr-professor-doktor-maximus-raumkopf\|Professor Doktor Maximus Raumkopf]], the property also served as the historic landing site of the Matterhorn cosmic meteorite, which contaminated the surrounding land with magical extraterrestrial matter upon impact. This extraterrestrial dust subsequently caused bizarre physical mutations in early miners and local wildlife residing on the property, adding a cosmic dimension to the site's anomaly history.
