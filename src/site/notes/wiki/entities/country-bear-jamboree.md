# Country Bear Jamboree

## Description

The [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Country Bear Jamboree/Country Bear Jamboree\|Country Bear Jamboree]] is a containment facility disguised as a musical stage show located in Frontierland. The bears featured in the attraction are actually the surviving subjects of a failed government super-soldier experiment conducted during the Apache Wars, which was led by [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] researcher [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Cadence Ritm\|Dr. Cadence Ritm]]. Instead of developing military prowess, the bears gained high intelligence, an affinity for musical performance, and an addiction to alcohol. After escaping their military handlers, the group was eventually secured by [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]], which established the permanent theatrical environment to safely contain them while accommodating their unique behaviors.

Dr. Cadence Ritm's original experiment attempted to utilize music as a tool to control wild animals, but this unexpectedly triggered the creation of the highly intelligent super-soldiers; the resulting rampage by the escaped bears ultimately forced a mass evacuation of the area via the [[Big Thunder Mountain Railroad\|Big Thunder Mountain Railroad]]. Today, while the bears' chaotic, alcohol-fueled singing risks causing localized instability, structured and harmonious music is actively utilized as a core component of their ongoing containment protocol.

This experiment was officially conducted under the government initiative known as the Project for Advanced Mammalian Soldiering. Within this program, the bears were trained using a combination of music and positive reinforcement techniques before the project ultimately escaped control.
