# Bureau of Gustatory Stability

## Description

The Bureau of Gustatory Stability (B.G.S.) is a specialized, secret division within the [[Society of Explorers and Adventurers\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers]] tasked with using culinary science to preserve the fabric of reality. Under the leadership of [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Commodore Flavius M. Palate\|Commodore Flavius M. Palate]], the Bureau historically developed biochemical stabilization buffers, such as the [[Dole Whip\|Dole Whip]], to counter localized reality failures. In addition to these culinary initiatives, the agency also manages administrative and municipal affairs on [[wiki/entities/xandar\|Xandar]]. Following the disruptions of [[Operation Celestial-Vex\|Operation Celestial-Vex]] and a distress signal from [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Star-Lord (Peter Quill)\|Star-Lord (Peter Quill)]], the Bureau became heavily entangled in local complaints alongside [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Cadence Ritm\|Cadence Ritm]]. This bureaucratic fallout indirectly drew the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Xandar Complaints/Guardians of the Galaxy\|Guardians of the Galaxy]] into the periphery of the Bureau's planetary operations.

As a clinical and regulatory department, the Bureau formalizes strict public-facing containment protocols—such as the W.H.I.P. protocol—to address supernatural and environmental volatility encountered during S.E.A. expeditions. In managing these crises, the leadership must also navigate the intellectual and social chaos of S.E.A. members like [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Cadence Ritm\|Professor Ritm]], maintaining a professional relationship of polite but firm exasperation characterized by official letters correcting Ritm's frequent misdeeds.
