# Dinosaur Institute

## Description

The [[Dinosaur Institute\|Dinosaur Institute]] is a key temporal research facility operated within the Society of Explorers and Adventurers ([[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]]). Originally serving as the primary base of operations for [[Dr. Grant Seeker\|Dr. Grant Seeker]], the facility housed the [[Time Rover\|Time Rover]] technology developed for [[Closed-Loop Temporal Observation\|Closed-Loop Temporal Observation]]. Under the direction of [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Dr. Helen Marsh\|Dr. Helen Marsh]], the institute ran sanctioned temporal projects until it was permanently closed following political intervention by [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm\|Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm]]. Conflicting accounts surround its closure: official lore states [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm\|Professor Ritm]] exposed Seeker's unauthorized attempts to transport a live dinosaur, while Marsh asserts Ritm's campaign mistakenly blamed her team's sanctioned work for park instability. Following the shutdown, S.E.A. implemented a costly structural cover-up, transforming the facility into an Indiana Jones attraction that covertly functions as a front for a secret [[chronal artifact retrieval system\|chronal artifact retrieval system]].
