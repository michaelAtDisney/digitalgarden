# Ego

## Description

Ego is an ancient and powerful cosmic entity known as a Celestial who manifests himself as an entire living planet. He is the biological father of [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Star-Lord (Peter Quill)\|Peter Quill]], passing down a unique physiology that established Quill's identity as a [[Human-Celestial hybrid\|Human-Celestial hybrid]]. This lineage briefly granted Quill god-like power to manipulate energy and matter. However, to prevent Ego from enacting a catastrophic scheme of cosmic devastation, Quill and the Guardians of the Galaxy fought and defeated him, resulting in the permanent loss of Quill's inherited celestial abilities.
