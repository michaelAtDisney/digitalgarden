# Professor Algernon G. Ritm

## Description

Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm, Ph.D., is the ambitious and pompous Lead Researcher of [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]] and a key member of the [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] (Society of Explorers and Adventurers). An intellectual elitist with an immense ego, Ritm presents himself as a meticulous scholar, typically seen wearing tweed jackets, bow ties, and a crisp pocket protector, sporting a neatly trimmed beard and a permanent self-satisfied smirk. He views himself as the only true scientific mind in S.E.A., believing his theories to be unassailable and a spot on the wall of S.E.A. greats to be his absolute birthright. His surname, pronounced "writ-um," serves as an in-universe pun on the word "algorithm," reflecting his highly clinical, formulaic approach to reality.

Despite his arrogant facade, Ritm harbors deep insecurities and routinely steals credit for the genuine scientific breakthroughs made by his brilliant research assistant, Dr. [[Evelyn Reed\|Evelyn Reed]]. His obsessive drive is heavily fueled by a college rivalry with his former roommate, [[Maximus Raumkopf\|Maximus Raumkopf]], which stems from a past betrayal over credit for a shared discovery. Additionally, Ritm is motivated by a desperate desire to redeem his family's legacy. This includes exposing the failures of his great-great-grandfather, Dr. [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Cadence Ritm\|Cadence Ritm]], whose naive leadership of a military experiment to create super-soldier bears ultimately resulted in the Country Bear Jamboree.

As a staunch rationalist, Ritm strongly rejects whimsical public narratives surrounding Disney parks, asserting instead that they are physical manifestations of severe spatial instability. He formally classified these locations as [[D.I.S.N.E.Y.\|D.I.S.N.E.Y.]] (Dimensional Instability Systems for Novelty, Entertainment, and Yield) sites, employing AI-augmented methodologies to analyze them. Under this framework, he stubbornly insists that the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Matterhorn\|Matterhorn]] is merely a natural, geological anomaly. He is also credited with the in-universe discovery of the [[W.H.I.P.\|W.H.I.P.]] (Weakened Harmonizing Inter-dimensional Procedure) protocol, and he frequently blames widespread dimensional instabilities on the "reckless" time travel technology of [[Dr. Grant Seeker\|Dr. Grant Seeker]].

As a central figure in [[The Project Team\|The Project Team]], Ritm operates with a profound blind spot regarding anomalous phenomena, dismissively categorizing music as a purely emotional, unscientific variable. While investigating the Paradise Gardens area, he classified the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/World Notes/World of Color\|World of Color]] portal as a "Hydro-Dimensional Flux Emitter" (or "Chromatic Gate") and attempted to enforce clinical, data-driven containment protocols. He holds deep contempt for his predecessor, [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Dr. N. Itely Show\|Dr. N. Itely Show]], accusing him of intellectual laziness and mocking his attempts to manipulate the portal using "liquid harmony." However, Ritm's rigid methodology was ultimately unable to stabilize the portal—a feat achieved only through Dr. Show's intuitive, music-based methods, leaving Ritm's theories "missing a beat." He was also intensely offended to discover that [[N Itely\|N Itely]] received a portrait from S.E.A. while his own portrait, [[Algernon G. Ritm's Portrait\|Algernon G. Ritm's Portrait]], was rejected, further fueling his disdain for what he considers a "diluted" modern S.E.A.

This intellectual arrogance blinds him to the true cause of the park's widespread temporal instability: the unauthorized time-travel excursions of [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Johnny Depp\|Johnny Depp]]. Instead, Ritm falsely attributes these disruptions to Dr. Grant Seeker, viewing Depp's actions as the ultimate act of reckless disregard for scientific integrity.

His research into these temporal anomalies deepened when, during a routine cataloging of Disney archival materials, he personally discovered and flagged an anomalous oil painting designated [[wiki/entities/arc-oil-pir-07a\|ARC-OIL-PIR-07A]]. The painting depicts a modern-dressed [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Johnny Depp\|Johnny Depp]] standing among 18th-century pirates, prompting Ritm to order a Level 4 analysis to investigate his theory of how retrocausal temporal distortions can retroactively influence creative works.

Within S.E.A.'s temporal and transport departments, Ritm also engages in a tense bureaucratic rivalry with [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Frank Walker\|Frank Walker]], who serves as a pragmatic, results-driven scientific foil to Ritm's massive ego and constant political posturing.

To further her own research, Dr. [[Evelyn Reed\|Evelyn Reed]] deliberately tolerates Ritm's pompous behavior and massive ego because his high-level [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] clearance grants her invaluable access to restricted resources and archives. In fact, it was Dr. Reed's diligent archival work, rather than Ritm's own efforts, that actually uncovered the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Cadence Ritm\|Cadence Ritm]] and Country Bear archives, despite Ritm positioning himself as the primary discoverer. This professional dynamic is marked by frequent friction, as Dr. Reed regularly vents her frustration with Ritm's self-importance through dry, passive-aggressive notes addressed directly to him.

This academic rivalry traces back to their time at the University of Tübongen, where Ritm and Raumkopf initially formed a highly complementary partnership; Ritm's rigid pragmatism and empirical structure served as a necessary balance to Raumkopf's wild, imaginative ideas before their spectacular falling-out over a shared discovery that both men claimed as their own.

His professional reputation is further strained by [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Commodore Flavius M. Palate\|Commodore Flavius M. Palate]], the Director of the Bureau of Gustatory Stability, who views Ritm's ego-driven work as tiresome and considers him a constant liability, frequently issuing polite but firm letters to correct his misdeeds. It was Commodore Palate himself who acted as the official authority blocking Ritm's ambitions, personally informing the professor that his self-commissioned portrait could not be hung in the S.E.A. hall.

The military experiment led by his ancestor Cadence Ritm was formally known as the Project for Advanced Mammalian Soldiering, the historical records of which were actively suppressed by the [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] to cover up the disastrous endeavor.

Ritm is also locked in a severe administrative rivalry with Dr. [[Helen Marsh\|Helen Marsh]], the former Director of the decommissioned Dinosaur Institute. Driven by intellectual arrogance, Ritm successfully campaigned for the shutdown of her institute by mistakenly blaming its sanctioned Time Rover temporal observation experiments for park-wide instability, an action that led to a costly [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] cover-up in the form of the Indiana Jones attraction. This earned him a powerful enemy in Marsh, who now sits on the S.E.A. Oversight Board as Ritm's direct administrative superior, actively using her bureaucratic influence to audit his work and enforce strict protocol as retribution.
