# Dr. Grant Seeker

## Description

[[wiki/entities/dr-grant-seeker\|Dr. Grant Seeker]] is a scientist who conducted unauthorized temporal experiments at the [[wiki/entities/dinosaur-institute\|Dinosaur Institute]]. He is the inventor of the [[wiki/entities/time-rover\|Time Rover]] technology, which enables physical travel to the past. His ambitious plans to transport a live dinosaur to the present were exposed by his academic rival, [[wiki/entities/professor-algernon-g-ritm\|Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm]]. This exposure resulted in the immediate closure of his research facility and the suppression of his scientific work. His efforts represent the dangerous temporal meddling that modern containment agencies must regulate.
