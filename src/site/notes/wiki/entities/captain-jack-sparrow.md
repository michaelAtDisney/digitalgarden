# Captain Jack Sparrow

## Description

Captain Jack Sparrow is an iconic pirate persona whose distinct style and aesthetic are adopted by [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Johnny Depp\|Johnny Depp]]. Depp presents himself in the fashion of this character, routinely mixing historical pirate garb with highly anachronistic modern accessories like sunglasses and a wristwatch. This blended, unusual appearance serves as a physical reflection of Depp's chaotic lifestyle and his frequent, spontaneous movements through history. Within this context, his imitation of this figure defines his public-facing identity as he utilizes [[Temporal Travel\|Temporal Travel]] to alter pirate lore.
