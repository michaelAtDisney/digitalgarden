# Core Compendium

## Description

The Core Compendium is a central repository of documentation and research logs utilized by the analysis team within [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]] to establish a stable source of truth. It outlines the D.I.S.N.E.Y. (Dimensional Instability Systems for Novelty, Entertainment, and Yield) initiative, asserting that Disney parks are built on top of points of [[Weakened Reality\|Weakened Reality]] where attractions function as containment sites managed by the clandestine [[Society of Explorers and Adventurers\|Society of Explorers and Adventurers]]. The compendium details internal conflicts, including [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm\|Professor Algernon "Al" G. Ritm]] taking credit for the discoveries of his assistant, [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Dr. Evelyn Reed\|Dr. Evelyn Reed]], and his bitter academic rivalry with [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Herr Professor Doktor Maximus Raumkopf\|Herr Professor Doktor Maximus Raumkopf]] over the extraterrestrial origin of the [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Matterhorn\|Matterhorn]]. It also documents containment failures and protocols: [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Johnny Depp\|Johnny Depp]] acts as a rogue temporal agent fracturing history at [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Chapters/Pirates of the Caribbean/Pirates of the Caribbean\|Pirates of the Caribbean]], historical ancestor [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Cadence Ritm\|Cadence Ritm]]'s failed bear-control music experiments created the [[Big Thunder Mountain Railroad\|Big Thunder Mountain Railroad]] anomaly, and [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/Character Profiles/Commodore Flavius M. Palate\|Commodore Flavius M. Palate]] uses the [[Dole Whip\|Dole Whip]] under the motto *Fortitudo Per Gustum* as a reality-stabilizing agent. Additionally, the log notes that the Dinosaur Institute's defunct chronal technology is being reclaimed by an Indiana Jones chronal retrieval system, while night-time [[50 AI Notebooks/Project Wallick - Disney History/Sources/World Notes/World of Color\|World of Color]] shows act as portals to alternate realities using harmonious music as a containment tool.
