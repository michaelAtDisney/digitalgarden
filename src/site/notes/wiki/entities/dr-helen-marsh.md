# Dr. Helen Marsh

## Description

Dr. Helen Marsh is a senior administrator within [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] and an active member of the [[S.E.A.\|S.E.A.]] Oversight Board. She formerly served as the Director of the [[Dinosaur Institute\|Dinosaur Institute]], where she led temporal research initiatives before the facility's eventual closure. Characterized by her pragmatic, highly disciplined, and protocol-driven approach, Dr. Marsh champions strict containment and bureaucratic oversight over individual scientific ambition. She harbors an intense professional rivalry with [[Professor Ritm\|Professor Ritm]], whom she holds directly responsible for the shutdown of her institute and the derailment of her temporal research career. Leveraging her high administrative standing, she actively enforces strict protocols to restrict Ritm's activities and maintains a tight bureaucratic leash on associated initiatives, including [[Project Wallick\|Project Wallick]].
